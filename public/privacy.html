Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Hiles Media (“we,” “us,” or “our”) films cinematic couples sessions in Houston, Texas. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit this site or reach out to book a session, how we use it, and the choices you have.

01 Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us. When you use the inquiry form on this site, your browser opens your own email app with a pre-filled message — we receive whatever you send, which typically includes:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your phone number, if you provide it
  • Details about your event — the occasion, date, location, and anything else you tell us

This website does not run its own database or store form submissions on a server; inquiries arrive as ordinary email in our inbox.

02 How we use your information

We use the information you share only to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and answer your questions
  • Prepare a quote and plan your session
  • Communicate with you before, during, and after your booking

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

03 Photos, video & your likeness

Our work involves filming and photographing you. How your footage may be used — including whether any of it may appear in our portfolio or on social media — is governed by the separate booking agreement you sign before your session, not by this website policy. If you have preferences about the public use of your images, tell us and we will honor them in that agreement.

04 Cookies & tracking

We use the Meta Pixel, a piece of tracking code from Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram), to measure how our advertising performs. The pixel uses cookies and may collect information such as the pages you view on this site, your device and browser, and your actions here, so we can understand how our ads perform and reach people who may be interested in our work. This information is shared with Meta and may be combined with data Meta already holds about you.

You can control this tracking through your Meta ad preferences, your browser’s cookie settings, or industry opt-out tools such as the Digital Advertising Alliance. Fonts are also loaded from Google Fonts, and the site may be served through a hosting provider that keeps basic, aggregate access logs for security and performance.

05 Third-party services

We rely on a few trusted providers to operate — for example, an email provider to receive and reply to your inquiry, a web host to serve this site, and Meta Platforms, Inc. for advertising measurement (see “Cookies & tracking” above). These providers process information only as needed to deliver their service and under their own privacy terms.

06 How long we keep it

We keep inquiry emails and project communications for as long as needed to serve you and to keep records of our work. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, and we will do so unless we are required to retain it.

07 Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have shared with us, or to stop contacting you. Just email us and we will take care of it.

08 Children’s privacy

This site is intended for adults booking sessions. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Note that couples sessions such as gender and baby reveals may involve filming families — that footage is always arranged directly with the adults who book us.

09 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “last updated” date at the top of this page.

10 Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? We’re happy to help.
